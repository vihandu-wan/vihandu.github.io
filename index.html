How I work

  1. Understand the problem. Learn how the current process works and what needs to improve.
  2. Design the solution. Define requirements, architecture, constraints and the simplest useful implementation.
  3. Build and validate. Implement the system, test its behaviour and demonstrate how it works.
  4. Improve through evidence. Use feedback, testing and real-world results to choose the next improvement.
