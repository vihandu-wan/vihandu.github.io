Practical software and business systems for organisations whose current tools don’t fit the way they work.

Current offerings

Delivery approach

  1. Discuss. We talk through the process and what hurts today.
  2. Scope. I propose the simplest useful solution, with clear requirements.
  3. Build. You see working software early.
  4. Review. We test it together and decide what to improve next.
